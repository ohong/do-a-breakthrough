# Contributing

Open an issue or pull request if the skill found something, failed in an
interesting way, or needs fixing. You do not need a math or research background.

## What to include

- The problem, its source, and what happened. Call it what it is: a possible new
  result, rediscovery, partial result, or failed attempt.
- The models, agents, tools, settings, and rough time or compute used.
- The exact starting prompt and follow-up nudges.
- The proof or counterexample, what failed, and what remains uncertain.
- Any checks, code, formal proof, or expert review. If no expert checked it, say
  **Not expert reviewed**.
- A link to any publishable model responses, visible reasoning, logs, or full
  transcript.
- For a correction, link the original claim and explain the error.

Only publish material you have permission to share. Get consent before sharing
another person's messages or identifying information, and remove secrets,
private data, confidential work, and personal information.

Do not extract or reconstruct hidden chain-of-thought. Share the reasoning the
model displayed, summaries, scratch work, and checks instead.

By contributing, you agree that your contribution is licensed under this
repository's MIT License and that you have the right to submit the included
material.
